Controls

Keyboard: WASD or arrows move the stick at full tilt, and arrows also press the D-pad. Hold Shift for a half stick (tilts and walking). J=A, K=B, L=Z, Q=L, E=R, Enter=Start, U/N/H/I = C-up/down/left/right, F = fullscreen (Esc leaves it).

Smash attack: press a direction and J (A) together, or flick the stick and press A within a few frames. Tilt: hold Shift, a direction and J. This is Smash 64: there is no smash charging by holding A. Holding does matter for DK's Giant Punch and Samus's Charge Shot (hold K, B), for repeating jabs (hold J) and for running or shielding.

Gamepad: standard mapping, A→A, B/X→B, LT/RT→Z, LB→L, RB→R, Y and right stick→C buttons, D-pad→D-pad, Start→Start. Left stick→analog stick (deadzone 15%, range ±80).
